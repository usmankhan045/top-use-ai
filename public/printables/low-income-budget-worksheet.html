🛡️ When Money Is Tight, Cover These First
1 Housing — keep a roof over your head
2 Utilities — electric, gas, water stay on
3 Food — groceries for you and your family
4 Transportation — getting to work or school
5 Minimum Debt Payments — protect your credit

WIC — food & nutrition for moms & kids
LIHEAP — help with heating & cooling bills
