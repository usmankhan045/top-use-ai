# Pinterest Pin Schedule and Prompts (First 10 Posts, 30 Pins)

Each of the 10 launch posts gets 3 pins (A, B, C), each a different image and hook pointing to the same blog URL. A post's 3 pins go out 2 days apart (A, then +2 days B, then +2 days C). Pins are staggered so you post about 6 pins on odd days and 4 on even days.

> Start "Day 1" only after the site is live on your domain and your Pinterest business account is set up (domain claimed, Rich Pins on, boards created). Pinterest needs a public URL. Generate images in ChatGPT with the prompts below.
>
> Rules: never put affiliate links in a pin (link to the blog post URL only), no link shorteners, keep to about 5 or 6 fresh pins per day on a new account, and never post a post's multiple pins on the same day.

## How to make pins that do NOT look AI generated

The generic "ChatGPT image" look (flat vector icons, glossy 3D blobs, symmetrical clip art, smooth gradients) is instantly recognizable and gets ignored or soft suppressed on Pinterest. These prompts instead ask for photo real, editorial lifestyle images, the kind a real blog would shoot. Tips baked into every prompt:

1. Photoreal editorial photography, real scenes and props, natural window light, shallow depth of field. No flat illustration or clip art.
2. A signature, consistent brand look (one sky blue accent object, a small warm pop, a frosted text band, a tiny corner wordmark) so all pins read as one branded set that is still unique per topic.
3. Short, bold headline text. If ChatGPT renders the text imperfectly, generate the photo with an empty text band and add the headline yourself in Canva (this also guarantees clean, on brand type).
4. Real screenshots beat everything. Where a prompt says "laptop screen shows X", drop in a genuine screenshot of the tool for maximum authenticity and zero AI tells.
5. Subtle film grain and natural imperfection so it reads as a real photo, not a render.

## Signature look (referenced as [LOOK] in every prompt)

> **[LOOK]** = "Photo real editorial Pinterest image, 1000x1500 vertical (2:3), shot like a premium lifestyle and tech blog: real scene, soft natural window light, shallow depth of field like a 50mm f/1.8 lens, authentic textures, faint film grain. NOT flat vector art, NOT clip art, NOT a 3D render. Airy, slightly muted color grade with one signature sky blue (#0369A1) accent object and a small warm amber (#F59E0B) pop. A clean frosted white horizontal band across the lower third (about 70 percent opacity) holds a bold, tight modern sans serif headline in near black. Tiny 'explainedaitools.com' in the bottom corner. Uncluttered, premium, scroll stopping."
