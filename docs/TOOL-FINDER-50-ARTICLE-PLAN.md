# Top Use AI: 50-Article SEO, AEO, and GEO Plan

Updated: October 22, 2026

## Editorial position

Top Use AI should not compete by publishing the longest lists. Its editorial promise is:

> Answer five practical questions and find AI tools that fit your job, budget, access requirements, and priorities.

Every article should do at least one of these jobs:

1. Answer a real selection question.
2. Publish original catalog data that other sites and AI answers can cite.
3. Explain an important tool-buying concept clearly.
4. Send the reader into a relevant, preconfigured finder.
5. Help a user choose between realistic workflows or constraints.

## Topic fields

- **Query family** is the group of searches the page should satisfy. It is not a claim about search volume.
- **Citation asset** is the original element that makes the article worth referencing in an AI answer or another publication.
- **Finder path** is the product destination that should be embedded and linked from the article.
- **Internal links** identifies the closest supporting pages in this plan.

## Internal-link architecture

### Core hubs

- `/tools` is the parent hub for all finder content.
- Every niche guide links to its corresponding `/tools/[niche]` finder.
- Every niche finder links back to its main decision guide.
- Articles 1 through 8 form the authority and methodology hub.

### Required linking rules

1. Every article links to one primary finder in the first useful decision section, not only at the end.
2. Every article links to its parent niche guide.
3. Every article links to two or three sibling articles that answer the reader's next likely question.
4. Every niche guide links to at least one pricing article, one access or privacy article, and one workflow article.
5. Links use descriptive anchor text. Avoid repeated anchors such as “click here” or “learn more.”
6. Existing relevant articles should be updated to link into these new hubs instead of creating competing pages for the same intent.
7. No article should be orphaned. Each new page needs at least two contextual inbound internal links at publication time.

## SEO requirements for every article

- One clear primary search intent and one canonical URL.
- A descriptive title and H1 that accurately summarize the page.
- A concise direct answer near the top.
- Logical H2 and H3 sections based on decisions, not arbitrary keyword variations.
- Original analysis, examples, testing, or catalog data.
- Primary-source citations for pricing, policies, limits, and product claims.
- Visible author, reviewed date, and substantive update history.
- Relevant Article and BreadcrumbList structured data.
- SoftwareApplication structured data only where the visible page and required fields genuinely support it.
- Meaningful image alt text and lightweight comparison graphics where they improve understanding.
- A short, specific finder CTA that preserves the article's context.
- A unique meta title and description written for the actual intent.

## AEO and GEO requirements for citation-worthy pages

1. **Use citable units.** Put important facts into short paragraphs and clearly labeled tables with stable headings.
2. **Publish the method.** Every original number must state the catalog size, inclusion rules, calculation date, and definitions.
3. **Make claims precise.** Prefer “312 of 1,196 cataloged tools had a verified permanent free option on October 22, 2026” over “many tools are free.”
4. **Separate fact from judgment.** Label catalog facts, vendor claims, testing observations, and editorial recommendations distinctly.
5. **Name entities consistently.** Use canonical tool names, categories, pricing labels, platforms, and URLs.
6. **Answer comparison questions directly.** Include “best for,” “not suitable for,” limitations, and the deciding requirement.
7. **Keep important content in server-rendered text.** Do not hide the only useful answer inside an image or client-only interaction.
8. **Maintain source freshness.** Pricing and feature tables need last-checked dates and scheduled re-verification.
9. **Use primary sources.** Link to official pricing, documentation, privacy, terms, and open-source repositories where applicable.
10. **Avoid fake FAQ padding.** Add questions only when they resolve a genuine follow-up intent.

## Technical visibility requirements for AI answers

- Allow `OAI-SearchBot` if the site should appear in ChatGPT search results. This control is independent from GPTBot training preferences.
- Keep Googlebot and Bingbot access open for indexable editorial pages.
- Confirm CDN and bot protection do not block approved search crawlers.
- Submit clean XML sitemaps containing canonical articles, niche pages, and tool profiles.
- Use IndexNow for fast notification when pricing reports and frequently updated pages change.
- Monitor Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
- Use Bing AI Performance reporting for citations, cited pages, grounding queries, topics, and citation-share trends when available.
- Track referral traffic from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot, Gemini, and other answer engines in analytics.
- Do not depend on `llms.txt` or invented “AI schema” as a visibility strategy. Search accessibility, indexability, useful text, clear sourcing, and original evidence remain the foundation.
- Do not index every finder combination. Curate only combinations with distinct intent and substantial editorial value.

## Measurement

Track these separately:

- Organic impressions, clicks, and non-branded queries
- Article-to-finder click rate
- Finder preset loads
- Question changes after entering from an article
- Shortlists created or shared
- Tool outbound clicks
- Returning users
- Referring domains and earned links
- AI referral sessions
- AI citations and cited-page coverage where platforms report it
- Pages with traffic but weak finder engagement
- Pages with finder engagement but weak search visibility

The primary content success metric is not raw pageviews. It is the number of qualified readers who enter a relevant finder, receive a useful shortlist, and continue to a tool or share the result.
