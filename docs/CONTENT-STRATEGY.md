## 0. The core system

```
        PINTEREST (primary)                 GOOGLE + AI ENGINES (compounding)
        3+ ChatGPT-made pins/article        /seo handles on-page SEO + GEO
                │                                     │
                ▼                                     ▼
   ┌─────────────────────────┐        ┌─────────────────────────────┐
   │  PIN MAGNETS (TOFU)      │  link  │  MONEY PAGES (BOFU)         │
   │  outcome listicles,      │───────▶│  reviews, "best for X",     │
   │  "make money", how-tos   │  down  │  "free alt to X", vs        │
   │  → email capture         │◀───────│  → affiliate links here     │
   └─────────────────────────┘  link   └─────────────────────────────┘
                │                up                    │
                ▼                                      ▼
         EMAIL LIST  ──────────────────────────▶  AFFILIATE + ADSENSE REVENUE
```

- **Pins never carry affiliate links** (safe, per your plan). Pins → blog article → affiliate link + AdSense **on the page**.
- **Pin magnets** pull Pinterest traffic; **money pages** convert + get cited by AI engines. Pin magnets link *down* to money pages; money pages link *up* to a use-case and *sideways* to a free download (email capture).
- **You can publish before you have affiliate links** — use the `/go/[tool]` redirect pattern (§7) so links are added centrally later.

**One-line strategy:** *Bank a large library of outcome-driven pin magnets and tool money-pages (multiplied from a small set of tools via the Angle Matrix), pin each with 3+ ChatGPT-designed pins for maximum outbound clicks, and monetize the pages with genuine, testing-backed affiliate recommendations — concentrated first on the faceless-creator wedge, then the high-value B2B tool clusters (SEO, lead-gen).*

## 2. Content multiplication — the Angle Matrix (your content bank engine)

**Principle you raised:** one tool → many posts. Each tool gets **spun into 6–8 posts** across different angles/intents, each targeting a different keyword and pinnable with its own hooks. A starter set of ~10 tools → **60–80 posts** of bank inventory before you repeat yourself.

### The Angle Matrix (apply to every tool)

| Angle | Post pattern | Type | Primary channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Roundup inclusion** | "[N] Best AI [category] Tools for [outcome]" (tool is a pick) | Pin magnet + money | Pinterest + Google |
| **Best-for-persona** | "Best AI [category] Tool for [persona/use-case]" → this tool wins | Money | Pinterest + Google |
| **Free / cheaper alternative** | "Free Alternatives to [Tool]" · "[Tool] but Cheaper" | Money | Google + Pinterest |
| **Head-to-head** | "[Tool] vs [Competitor]" (with a surprising winner) | Money | Google/AI |
| **Standalone review** | "[Tool] Review: I Tested It for [N] Days" | Money | Google/AI |
| **Is-it-worth-it** | "Is [Tool] Worth It in 2026?" | Money (BOFU) | Google/AI |
| **How-to / outcome** | "How to [achieve outcome] with [Tool]" | Pin magnet | Pinterest |
| **Pricing explained** | "[Tool] Pricing: Free vs Paid (What You Actually Get)" | Money (BOFU) | Google |

**Content-bank rules:**
- Write in **batches** (e.g., pick one tool, generate 4–6 of its angles at once), bank them as drafts, then **schedule-publish 3–4/week**. Never dump everything live at once — a steady stream signals a healthy site and feeds Pinterest fresh URLs.
- **Don't cannibalize:** each angle must target a *distinct* keyword/intent. "Best AI voice tools" (category listicle) and "Is ElevenLabs worth it" (single-tool BOFU) don't compete; two near-identical "best AI voice tool" posts do.
- **Interlink the matrix:** the roundup links to each tool's review; each review links up to the roundup and to a "best-for-persona" post. This builds the topical cluster AI engines and Google reward.
- **Refresh, don't just add** — flagship money pages get re-tested/updated every 30–45 days (AI-tool prices/models change monthly); bump the visible "Last updated" date.

## 3. Content format mix for MAXIMUM outbound clicks

You asked me to pick the best formats — here's the ranked mix (highest Pinterest outbound-click potential × winnable for a new domain). Weight production toward the top.

| Rank | Format | Why it gets the click | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Outcome listicle** "[N] AI tools for [specific outcome]" | Pinterest's native save-format; curiosity + utility | "7 AI Tools to Start a Faceless YouTube Channel (Free)" |
| 2 | **"Free / cheapest alternative to [tool]"** | Reader knows the brand, wants cheaper → clicks for the answer | "5 Free Alternatives to Midjourney (That Actually Work)" |
| 3 | **Outcome/proof** "I made $X / I tested N for N days" | Curiosity gap + credibility; high saves | "I Tested 7 AI Voice Tools for 14 Days — Only 2 Sound Human" |
| 4 | **Persona "best for"** "Best AI [x] for [persona]" | Specificity dodges saturated head terms; matches Pinterest boards | "Best AI Writing Tools for Overwhelmed Teachers" |
| 5 | **How-to / tool-stack** "How to [outcome] with AI" | Actionable; funnels to money pages | "How to Make Faceless Videos With AI (Full Tool Stack)" |
| 6 | **"X vs Y" (surprising winner)** | Great for Google/AI + affiliate; pin only with a twist hook | "ElevenLabs vs Murf: I Expected ElevenLabs to Win. It Didn't." |
| 7 | **Single review / is-it-worth-it / pricing** | Bottom-funnel Google + AdSense dwell; low Pinterest | "Is Synthesia Worth It in 2026?" |

**Headline formula for pins & titles:** `[Curiosity trigger] + [specific number/outcome] + [named persona or tool] + [tension/surprise]`. Lead with outcomes and specificity; avoid vague clickbait (Pinterest rewards *specific*).

## 4. Pin strategy (ChatGPT-generated, built for outbound clicks)

You'll generate pins with **ChatGPT image gen**. That's workable, but Pinterest now **soft-suppresses images it labels as AI** — so the rule is: make pins read as **designed graphics, not AI illustrations**.

**3+ pins per article, each a distinct creative + keyword** (all link to the same blog URL), spaced **72h–1 week apart**, rotated across relevant boards:

| Pin | Angle | Hook example |
|---|---|---|
| A | Primary keyword | "7 Best AI Voice Tools (2026)" |
| B | Outcome / secondary kw | "AI Voices That Actually Sound Human" |
| C | Pain-point / long-tail | "How I Voiced 10 Videos Without Talking" |

**Design specs:** 1000×1500 (2:3 vertical) · text hook **4–8 words, big & high-contrast** · one focal point legible at thumbnail size · brand colors (Azure `#0369A1`, Amber `#F59E0B`) and fonts (Space Grotesk / Inter) for a consistent, *designed* look · text overlay ~20–40% of the pin.

**ChatGPT pin-prompt template:**
> "Create a 1000×1500 vertical Pinterest pin graphic (2:3). Clean, modern **flat-design infographic** style — NOT photorealistic AI art. Background: soft sky-blue gradient (#F6FAFD → #E4EFFA). Bold headline text at top in a geometric sans-serif, dark navy (#0F1B2A): '**[4–8 word hook]**'. Below, a simple [icon row / numbered list / device mockup] representing [topic]. Small amber (#F59E0B) accent. Leave a clean footer band with the text 'explainedaitools.com'. High contrast, mobile-legible, minimal, professional — looks like a designed brand template, not generated art."

**Anti-suppression tactics:** favor **text-forward / infographic / mockup** designs over illustrative AI scenes; where possible **composite a real tool screenshot** into the pin (screenshots are the strongest converter *and* read as authentic); keep a consistent branded template so pins look like a designed system. Vary the 3 pins' layout, not just the color.

**Boards (10–15, keyword-named):** AI Writing Tools & Prompts · AI Art & Image Generators · Faceless Video / Faceless YouTube · AI Side Hustles & Make Money · AI Voice & Text-to-Speech · Best AI Tools (roundups) · AI for Productivity · AI SEO Tools · AI Prompts & Templates · AI for Beginners. Each needs 20–30 pins + a keyword description before you push it.

**Account rules:** Business account → **claim explainedaitools.com** + enable **Rich Pins** first · **1–5 fresh pins/day** for a new account (not 15–30) · keywords from **Pinterest Trends**, not Google · no shorteners/cloaked links in pins · ~80% helpful / ≤20% promotional.

## 8. Honest risks & expectations
- **Pinterest ramp is 3–6 months**; tech under-indexes on click-rate — consistency + pin design quality matter most.
- **AI-generated pins risk soft-suppression** — mitigate with designed/infographic/screenshot-composite pins, not AI-art scenes.
- **GEO is mostly zero-click** — value is brand + being the *named, tested* source + mid-funnel long-tail; capture the click AI can't resolve (full tables, screenshots, "how we tested").
- **B2B categories (SEO/lead-gen/productivity) won't get Pinterest traffic** — they earn via Google/AI + high commissions.
- **Affiliate terms are volatile in 2026** — re-verify every rate at signup and each refresh; publish now with `/go/` redirects and fill links in later.
- **AdSense first, affiliate density later** — thin affiliate pages jeopardize approval and rankings.
