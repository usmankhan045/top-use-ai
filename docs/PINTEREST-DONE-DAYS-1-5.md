**Archive. Do not re-upload.** These 30 pins went live in the first week of
pinning and are kept only as a record of what is on which board.

this batch: three prompts covered 231 of 240 pins, so the images rendered
near-identically and the titles fell into repeating shapes. Everything from
Day 6 onward is written per-pin instead, with no shared template.
