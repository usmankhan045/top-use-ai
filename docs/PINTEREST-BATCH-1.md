# Pin Schedule V2 — Outcome-Led Rebuild

Replaces the previous pin strategy entirely. Built on Pinterest keyword research
(September 2026) and a new four-template design system.

---

## What changed and why

**The finding that drove everything:** Pinterest autocomplete only fires on terms
with real search volume. Testing 13 seed terms:

| Returned autocomplete | Returned nothing |
|---|---|
| side hustle ideas | make money with ai |
| passive income ideas | ai side hustle |
| work from home | free ai tools |
| digital products to sell | ai art |
| etsy shop ideas | chatgpt prompts |
| faceless youtube | midjourney prompts |

**"AI" as a search term is effectively dead on Pinterest.** People search outcomes,
not tools. Every pin below leads with the outcome; AI is the method, mentioned
inside the article rather than in the headline.

**Modifier types Pinterest surfaced**, used to generate three distinct pins per post:

- Audience — for beginners · for women · for moms · for students · for teens · for men
- Location — at home · online
- Platform — on etsy · canva · gumroad · on pinterest
- Qualifier — free · legit · aesthetic · 2026

## Schedule

- **6 pins per day**, each from a different post
- Pin **B** for a post goes out **+2 days** after pin A, pin **C** at **+4 days**
- 80 posts x 3 pins = **240 pins over 40 days**
- Drafts scheduled Sept 3 onward get their pins first so each post is alive when it publishes

## Design templates

Four templates. Brand tokens: Graphite `#22202E`, Electric Lime `#D6FF3F`,
Warm Paper `#FBFAF6`, Slate `#5C5A68`.

| Template | Use for | Share |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Dark List** | Roundups, comparisons, how-tos | ~35% |
| **B — Bold Number** | Numbered listicles | ~20% |
| **C — Split Card** | Category overviews, beginner guides | ~20% |
| **D — Showcase** | AI art, logos, image and video tools | ~25% |

Each post's three pins use different templates so the creatives are genuinely
distinct rather than colour swaps.

## Rules

- **1000x1500 (2:3)**, always
- Headline **4-8 words**, readable at 240px wide
- **Never lead with a tool name** in the headline. Outcome first
- Each post's 3 pins use **different keywords and different templates**
- Descriptions: 2-3 sentences, front-load the keyword, end with "Tested, not hyped."
- Alt text describes the image for accessibility, not keyword stuffing
- Link is always the full `https://www.topuseai.com/blog/<slug>` URL

# BATCH 1 · DAYS 1-14

Every pin is complete and ready to copy. Each field is its own block — click the
copy icon, paste, nothing to fill in.

**Publishing pattern:** 6 pins per day. Each post gets 3 pins using 3 different
templates. Pin **A** publishes on its day, pin **B** two days later, pin **C**
four days later, so every post stays alive across a week.
